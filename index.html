NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between an Online MBA and an Online MCA?

An Online MBA focuses on business, management and leadership. An Online MCA focuses on computing and technology. Compare each curriculum with your previous education and career goals before choosing.

How many specialisations are listed for the Online MBA?

The programme card on this page lists eight specialisations: Banking & Financial Services, Business Analytics, Operations Management, Supply Chain Management, Finance Management, Human Resource Management, Information Technology Management and Marketing Management. Confirm current availability with GLA Online.

How do I check whether I am eligible?

Submit an enquiry and ask the admissions team to assess your previous qualification against the current eligibility criteria for your chosen programme.

What do the fees shown on this page mean?

The displayed figures are illustrative net tuition after an assumed ₹8,000 cashback, calculated as 2 × ₹4,000 EMI. The original figures shown are ₹97,000 for MBA and ₹86,000 for MCA. Cashback eligibility, payment schedule, additional charges and current offers must be confirmed with the university.

Does the programme guarantee a job or promotion?

No. A qualification may support career development, but employment, promotion and salary outcomes depend on many factors and are not guaranteed.

What happens after I submit the enquiry form?

The admissions team can contact you to discuss programme options, eligibility, fees and the next steps. Submitting an enquiry is not the same as receiving admission.

Which documents may be required for admission?

Requirements vary. Ask the admissions team for the current checklist for your programme before submitting documents or making a payment.
